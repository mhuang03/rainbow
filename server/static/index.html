        How to Play

        The goal

        Score the most points by taking the best piles in the middle of the table.

        The deal

        The deck has ten cards of each rank from 1 to 6. Everyone gets a hand, and a few cards are dealt face up as the first piles. With three players, 15 cards are set aside unseen.

        Playing a trick

        Each trick, everyone plays once, in turn:

        • 4 a single card,
        • 555 a set of equal cards, or
        • 234 a run of consecutive ranks.

        Once someone plays a set, the rest of the trick may only play singles or sets; once someone plays a run, only singles or runs.

        Taking piles

        When everyone has played, the plays are ranked: more cards beat fewer, then the higher top card, then the earlier play. The best play takes the most valuable pile, the next best the next pile, and so on. The cards just played become the next piles.

        332 cards: besttakes 6
        51 card, highertakes 4
        21 card, lowertakes 2

        The end

        The game ends after a trick in which two players have run out of cards. Most points wins.

        With two players, each plays twice per trick, and a face-down reserve of 14 cards is picked up when your hand first runs out. Running out again ends the game, and your opponent scores a point for each card left in their hand.

        Controls

        Tap cards to select them, then Play. Double-tap a card to select all cards of that rank. An arrow (→ 6) shows the pile a play would take if the trick ended now. Against the computer, Suggest asks the strongest computer player for a move to consider.

        Playing comfortably

        Settings has a slower or faster computer, reduced motion, sound, and a light or dark theme; your system's high-contrast setting is followed too. Everything works by keyboard, and screen readers hear each play and whose turn it is. Watch a game shows four computer players with their hands open, to learn by watching.
