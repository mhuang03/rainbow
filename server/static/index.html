          How to play

          The deck has ten cards of each rank, 1 to 6. Each trick, everyone plays once (twice in a 2-player game): a single, a set of equal cards, or a run of consecutive ranks. Once a set is played, only singles and sets may follow; once a run is played, only singles and runs.

          When the trick ends, plays are ranked: more cards beat fewer, then the higher top card, then the earlier play. In that order, players take the point piles in the middle, most valuable first. The played cards then form the next piles. The game ends after a trick in which two players have run out of cards; the most points wins.

          With two players, each plays twice per trick, and a face-down reserve of 14 cards is picked up when a hand first runs out. Running out a second time ends the game, and the other player scores a point for each card left in their hand.
